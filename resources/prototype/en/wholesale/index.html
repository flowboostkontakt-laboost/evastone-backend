Frequent questions

What retailers ask before the first conversation.

How large is the first order?

First order from 4–5 sets, or the equivalent in rings and earrings. After that you restock from 1 piece, across the full size range. We carry half of the stock risk with you.

Does oxidised silver turn black over time?

At EvaStone oxidation is a designed element, not ageing: it sits in the recesses of the surface structure and traces the pattern. What changes with wear is the patina on the raised areas — a polishing cloth takes it back. A care guide comes with every delivery.

Do I get territory protection?

One partner per territory, agreed in writing. The protection covers the territory, not exclusivity on the brand. Whether your territory is open we settle in the first conversation — the map of occupied and open territories is public.

What is in the starter pack?

Boxes for rings, earrings, necklaces and pendants, leaflets, 4–5 display trays, a display stand and frames for the product photo. The starter pack is free and arrives with the first delivery — together with the first quarterly content pack.

How quickly do you deliver?

We confirm the delivery date individually with each order.

Which stones do you work with?

From a closed list: rough and black diamond cube, topaz, white and orange sapphire, pink tourmaline, emerald and tanzanite. Every stone is named with its cut — round, square, rectangular or navette.
