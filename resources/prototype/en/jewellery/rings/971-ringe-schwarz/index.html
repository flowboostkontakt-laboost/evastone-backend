Questions about this model

How do I ask about this model?

Quote model number 971SD-AK at the boutique. We do not sell directly to end customers — your stockist checks availability.

Which sizes are available?

The full size range 15–23. Sizes outside the standard range we agree individually.

Does the surface change over time?

Oxidation sits in the recesses of the structure and is a designed element, not ageing. The patina on raised areas can be taken back with a polishing cloth.

Which stone is used?

We work with black diamond cube from a closed list of eight gemstones. Every stone is named together with its cut.

Ring 971SD-AK in 925 silver, gilding and oxidation. The stone is black diamond cube in a square cut. The surface structure follows the design and repeats in no second piece. Made in our own goldsmith workshop and available through independent boutiques — ask there about availability quoting the model number.
