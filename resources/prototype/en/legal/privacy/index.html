6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests — including direct marketing, at any time and without giving reasons. Consent may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office. An objection raised in one channel is honoured across all channels.

7. Automated decision-making

We do not take decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you. Business contacts are segmented by trade, country and stage of conversation; this serves the selection of content, not the assessment of individuals.

8. Artificial intelligence

We use AI tools when producing marketing content, translations and graphic elements; materials containing generated elements are labelled. Details in the section „Use of artificial intelligence". We do not pass personal data to generative models for training.
