Stone of the month · 2026-09

Tanzanite

Tanzanite is EvaStone's stone of the month: a natural gemstone from the closed list of eight, set in 925 silver. Its colour shifts between blue and violet depending on the viewing angle. Every setting is chased by hand — no piece repeats the surface of another. Available through independent boutiques, not sold directly.

The stone

Tanzanite is one of the few gemstones that change colour with the viewing angle: blue turns violet depending on how the light falls. That is why it is on our closed list — a stone that moves suits a surface that never repeats.

The cut

We set tanzanite in round and square cuts. The round cut gathers the colour shift into a single point; the square shows it in planes. Both sit in a bezel setting that protects the stone and keeps the colour calm.

The setting

The setting is cast and then chased by hand. The surface structure follows the design, but the hand never repeats itself — which is why every piece carries its own number and an NFC certificate.

Frequent questions

Is tanzanite suitable for every day?
At 6–7 on the Mohs scale, tanzanite is softer than sapphire. For daily wear: take it off for housework and gardening, and store it separately from harder stones. The bezel setting protects the stone's edges.
Why does the stone change colour?
Tanzanite shows different colours depending on the crystal direction — from blue to violet. That is a property of the stone itself, not an effect of shop lighting. You see the shift most clearly in daylight.
Will my piece look like the photo?
The stone yes, the setting not quite: every surface is chased individually and repeats in no second piece. That is why each piece carries a model number and an NFC certificate with the details of exactly your piece.
Where can I see the tanzanite models?
In our partners' independent boutiques — the stockist map shows where. We do not sell directly to end customers.
